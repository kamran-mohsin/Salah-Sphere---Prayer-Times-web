Islamic Calendar

The Prophet ﷺ encouraged fasting on specific days outside Ramadan for immense reward.

Monday & Thursday Fasting

Weekly Sunnah — "Deeds are presented on Monday and Thursday, and I love for my deeds to be presented while fasting." (Tirmidhi)

Ayyam al-Beed (White Days)

13th, 14th & 15th of every Lunar Month — Equal to fasting the entire year in spiritual reward.

Monthly Sunnah

6 Days of Shawwal & Day of Ashura

Fasting Ramadan followed by 6 days of Shawwal is like fasting a lifetime. Ashura expiates previous year sins.

Supplications for Fasting

Suhoor Intention (Niyyah)
وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ
"Wa bisawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri ramadan"
"And I intend to keep the fast tomorrow for the month of Ramadan."
اور میں نے رمضان کے کل کے روزے کی نیت کی۔
Iftar Dua (Breaking Fast)
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَىٰ رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ
"Allahumma inni laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa 'ala rizq-ika aftartu"
"O Allah! I fasted for You and I believe in You and I break my fast with Your sustenance."
اے اللہ! میں نے تیرے لیے روزہ رکھا اور تجھ پر ایمان لایا اور تیرے ہی رزق سے افطار کیا۔
